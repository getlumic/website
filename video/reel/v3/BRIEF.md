# lumic film v3 — 40 seconds, for get-lumic.com

Rebuilt from scratch on 2026-10-01 as pure motion design. Nothing in it is photographed, licensed, or generated by an
image or video model: every frame is drawn in code. A small 3D engine (perspective camera, painter's-order boxes,
floor-plane drawing) renders the world on a canvas; the type and the labels are HTML on top, positioned by projection.
Style: Quiet Tech, the same as the site and the v1 film. Indigo / near-black / white, Instrument Sans with Instrument
Serif italic accents, the orb wordmark, hairlines and soft shadows.

## The idea
The business as a blueprint. One light (the lumic orb) travels through it from the first frame to the last.

| Time | Scene | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | The page | The orb ignites on a flat white page and moves aside. "Your systems hold the data. Your team still does the work *by hand.*" |
| 3.85 | The business | The headline lifts; the page tilts into a 3D floor; six steps of a business rise as white blocks. Paper piles up on them and is carried between them by hand. The light visits each step. |
| 7.0 | We come in | The camera dives with the light into the business; it touches down on the first step. "We come in." / "We work on site, beside your team." |
| 10.0 | We learn | The light traces the real route as a long-exposure trail: email inbox → printed out → keyed in again → waiting for a signature (sent back once) → spreadsheet → report, by hand. Timestamps across a week. "6 hand-offs · 5 days". |
| 14.0 | We automate | The blocks glide into one straight workflow; the trail straightens with them; the paper clears. Only one step is an AI agent; the others are an integration, a person, an automation, a dashboard. One invoice runs through; a person clicks Approve. |
| 19.0 | Its own computer | A small computer rises in front of the workflow; the light flies into it and becomes its status light; every step is wired into it and light flows in. "Each AI agent runs on its own *dedicated computer.*" |
| 23.0 | Kept working | A monthly ring around the computer; a comet laps it and lights each check: tests passed, a change found and fixed, upgraded to a new model. "And we keep it *working.*" |
| 26.5 | Scale | The camera pulls back and up: the workflow is one of a city of workflows. The light spreads outward block to block along links. "In production, *every day.*" / "Dozens of workflows, and counting." |
| 29.7 | Night | The sky passes through lavender and indigo to night; the city glows. The connector fades up: "The tool we bring into *every job.*" AI assistant → MCP → your systems. Coming soon. |
| 35.0 | End card | An iris closes onto the light; it becomes the dot of the wordmark. "AI, deployed into your business and *kept working.*" get-lumic.com. The last 0.5 s holds (poster frame). |

## Rules (site briefs, 2026-09-30)
No prices. No system, client, or industry names. Never "FDE" or "forward-deployed engineering". Not every deliverable
is AI. Sample figures and steps are labelled "Illustration". Side lines stay off the site.

## How it is built
- `reel.html` is deterministic: `window.seek(t)` draws the exact frame for time `t`. Scene starts live in one table
  (`S`, on the 120 BPM grid); the camera is a list of keys (`CK`) interpolated with Hermite curves (holds, or
  continuous through a key). The page exports its own hit points as `window.CUES`.
- `score.py` is the v2 composition on the v1 synthesis engine (no samples, no licensed audio), re-cued to this picture:
  F# minor while the work is done by hand, A major once it is automated and kept working. Normalised to -16 LUFS.
- `python3 video/reel/render.py --v3` renders 1200 frames, scores them to `window.CUES`, and encodes MP4 + WebM +
  poster into `video/reel/out/v3/` for review. Add `--out public/video` to publish.
