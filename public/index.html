The problem

Your ERP holds the data. Your team still does the work by hand.

Most growing businesses run on capable systems and manual processes. Reports are assembled from exports. Invoices are keyed from the inbox. The same figures are reconciled across three systems every month.

  • 01

    Reporting takes hours.

    Exports, pivot tables, and copy-paste, every week and every month-end.

  • 02

    The inbox is a job.

    Invoices, payroll, status updates, and documents take a full-time role to process.

  • 03

    Enterprise BI does not fit.

    Tableau, Power BI, and Snowflake assume a data team. Most mid-size companies do not have one.

  • 04

    Spreadsheets become the system.

    Each department keeps its own version. The totals disagree, and decisions wait.
