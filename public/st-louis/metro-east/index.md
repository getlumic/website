## Any process, one agent at a time.

Each agent does one back-office job end to end, inside the systems you already run.

### AP automation

The agent reads each vendor bill from email, matches it to the purchase order, and flags a price difference before anyone pays. A person approves it, and nobody retypes it.

- Vendor bills and PO matching
- A person approves each entry

[AP automation](https://get-lumic.com/services/ap-automation/)

### Cash receipts and collections

Remittances and ACH detail, read and keyed into the ERP. Collections emails and statements drafted from live aging, ready when the day starts.

- Cash receipts keyed for approval
- A person sends every collections email

[Cash receipts and collections](https://get-lumic.com/services/cash-receipts-and-collections/)

### Payroll

The agent builds the payroll journal entry from your payroll reports and checks every line. The controller reviews it and posts it.

- Accruals and reversals
- Posted only on the controller’s click

[Payroll](https://get-lumic.com/services/payroll/)

An agent prepares the recurring month-end entries and runs the tie-outs. Every entry waits unposted until the controller posts it.
