Questions

AI consulting in St. Louis, in plain answers.

  • Does lumic offer AI consulting in St. Louis?

    Yes. lumic is based in St. Louis, and its FDEs come on site across Greater St. Louis, Missouri and Illinois. The work starts with a free consultation, then an on-site diagnostic of the back-office jobs your team does by hand.

  • What does lumic automate?

    Back-office jobs, one agent per job: vendor bills and PO matching, cash receipts and collections, the payroll journal entry, month-end close entries, and live dashboards and reporting.

  • How is lumic different from an AI consultant?

    A consultant advises and leaves. lumic's FDEs build the agent inside your own systems and stay to run it after launch, for a monthly fee.

  • Who approves what the agent does?

    A person on your team. The agent makes the entry, and a person checks it and approves it before anything posts. The final post stays in your hands.

  • What does AI consulting and automation cost?

    Free consultation. On-site work $1,500/day, credited toward phase one. Managed agents from $1,000/month. Full build-and-run from $2,500–6,000/month. Final pricing depends on your workflow and systems. These figures are starting points, not quotes. Details: Pricing.

  • Which areas does lumic serve?

    Greater St. Louis: St. Louis City, St. Louis County, St. Charles County, Jefferson County and Franklin County in Missouri; Madison County and St. Clair County in Illinois. lumic travels for the right fit. The area in full: St. Louis.
