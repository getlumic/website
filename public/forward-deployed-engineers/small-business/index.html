Forward Deployed Engineers for small and mid-size businesses.

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) works on site with a business, builds inside its own systems, and stays to run what was built. lumic’s FDEs have finance backgrounds. They build one back-office AI agent at a time, inside the systems your team already runs, and keep it working. A person on your team approves every entry before it posts.

Book a call What an FDE does

An FDE works on site, builds, and stays.

  2. 01

    Works on site.

    lumic’s FDEs work beside your team where the work happens. They map your systems, your data, and the manual steps that take the most time.

  3. 02

    Builds.

    The team builds an agent for one job, connects it to your systems, and sets up the approval step. Your team tests it on real work before it goes live.

  4. 03

    Stays.

    Once an agent is live, lumic runs it for a monthly fee. The team watches it, tests it every month, fixes what breaks, and upgrades it as AI improves.

Not another hire. Not a software license.

A smaller business needs the work done, not another tool to set up or another role to train. An FDE brings the build to you, inside the systems you already run, and stays to run it.

  • No new product to learn.

    Each agent works on its own dedicated computer, under its own login, inside the systems you already run, whether that’s an ERP, accounting software, email, spreadsheets or cloud apps.

  • Nothing to configure yourself.

    lumic does not sell software to configure yourself; the team stays to run what it builds.

  • Finance people who code.

    lumic’s team has done these back-office jobs by hand, then built the agents that do them.

FDEs for a smaller business, in plain answers.

  • Is an FDE a fit for a smaller business?

    Yes. A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) fits a business with people doing repetitive work by hand, that wants it done inside the systems the team already uses, with a person approving what matters. A smaller business gets the same on-site build and the same team that stays to run it.

  • How is an FDE different from a hire or a software license?

    lumic does not sell software to configure yourself. lumic’s Forward Deployed Engineers build the agent inside the systems you already run, and the team stays to run it for a monthly fee. A person on your team approves every entry before it posts.
