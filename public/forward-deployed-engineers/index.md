FDE or consultant

## A consultant advises. An FDE builds, and stays.

For a business that wants back-office work done by AI, the difference is who is left holding the work after launch. With lumic, it is lumic.

### On site.

lumic’s FDEs work beside your team at your place of business, where the work happens. They map your systems, your data, and the manual steps that take the most time.

### Inside your own systems.

Each agent works on its own dedicated computer, under its own login, inside the systems you already run, whether that’s an ERP, accounting software, email, spreadsheets or cloud apps. No new product to learn.

### Stays after launch.

Once an agent is live, lumic runs it for a monthly fee. The team watches it, tests it every month, fixes what breaks, and upgrades it as AI improves.

### Finance people who code.

Most FDE teams are engineers who learn finance on the job. lumic’s team has done these back-office jobs by hand, then built the agents that do them.
