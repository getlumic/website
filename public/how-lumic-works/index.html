How lumic works: Forward Deployed Engineers, one job at a time.

FDE stands for Forward Deployed Engineer. lumic’s FDEs are engineers with finance backgrounds who come to you, learn how the work is really done, build AI into one job at a time, and keep it working. lumic does not sell software to configure yourself; the team stays to run what it builds.

Book a call The four steps

lumic at a glance.

  • What it is

    A custom AI implementation firm. Its Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) build AI into the processes and systems a business already runs, then keep it working on a monthly retainer.

  • Where

    St. Louis, Missouri. On site across Greater St. Louis on both sides of the river, including the Metro East in Illinois. lumic travels for the right fit.

  • Who it is for

    Owner-run and mid-size businesses with repetitive work done by hand, for example manufacturers, distributors and service firms.

  • What it builds

    AI agents, automations and live dashboards for the order desk, email, vendor bills, cash receipts, payroll entries, month-end and reporting.

  • How it is controlled

    A person approves what matters before anything posts, and every action is logged.

  • Systems

    lumic works with the ERP, accounting, email and spreadsheet tools a team already uses: Systems lumic works with.

  • Pricing

    Free consultation.
    On-site work $1,500/day, credited toward phase one.
    The build is a separate, one-time cost, priced in the written proposal.
    Managed AI from $250 to $5,000 a month, depending on scope.

  • How to start

    A 20‑minute call, an on-site day, then a written proposal. hello@get-lumic.com

Approval, access and data

Gated by the people who know the work.

  • The final post stays with your team.

    An agent prepares the work. The people on your team who know it approve it before it posts. Dashboards only read.

  • It touches only its job.

    lumic gives each agent access to the screens and records its job needs, and nothing more. Every action it takes is logged.

  • It runs where it fits.

    Each agent works on its own dedicated computer, wherever fits your business, under its own login, inside the systems you already run. To read a document, it uses a cloud AI model.

  • It makes the entry itself.

    The agent enters the work in your systems, the same records a clerk would key. A person approves it, and nobody retypes it.

Why lumic

Forward Deployed Engineers who’ve closed the books.

lumic’s FDEs have finance backgrounds. They don’t just deploy agents; they know which back-office jobs are worth automating because they’ve done them.

Every engagement is run by FDEs with finance training, backed by a bench of agents that do the volume.

  • Finance people who code.

    Most FDE teams are engineers who learn finance on the job. lumic’s team knows the work: it has done these back-office jobs by hand, then built the agents that do them.

  • Is lumic the right fit?

    lumic fits a business with people doing repetitive work by hand, that wants it done inside the systems the team already uses, with a person approving what matters. lumic may not be the right fit for buyers who want a self-serve software license to set up themselves, or an agent that acts with no person approving.
