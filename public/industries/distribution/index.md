## The back-office jobs a distributor does by hand.

lumic’s FDEs start beside the person who does each job today, then build one agent per job, inside the systems the team already runs. A person approves each entry.

### Cash receipts

The agent reads remittances and ACH detail and keys them into the ERP for a person to approve.

Runs in: the ERP and the bank

- A person approves each entry

### Collections

From live aging, the agent drafts the collections email and the statement for each past-due customer. A person reviews each one and sends it.

## Gated by the people who know the work.

### Gated by the people who know the work.

An agent prepares the work. The people on your team who know it approve it before it posts. The final post stays in your hands. Dashboards only read.

### It touches only its job.

lumic gives each agent access to the screens and records its job needs, and nothing more. Every action it takes is logged.

### It runs where it fits.

Each agent works on its own dedicated computer, wherever fits your business, under its own login, inside the systems you already run. To read a document, it uses a cloud AI model.

lumic builds it, and keeps it working.
