# Month-end entries prepared and tied out. The controller posts them.

An agent reads the month’s activity from your ERP, prepares the recurring entries a controller builds by hand every month, and runs the tie-outs. Every entry lands unposted, and the controller reviews and posts each one. lumic builds it on site and keeps it working.

[Book a call](https://cal.com/lumic/20-minute-call) How it works

How it works

## Read, prepare, tie out. A person posts.

### It reads the period.

The agent reads the month’s activity straight from the ERP. Reading is all it does at this stage.

### It prepares and ties out.

It prepares the recurring entries and runs the tie-outs before anything is approved.

### The controller posts.

Every entry lands unposted. The controller reviews each one and posts it. Posting stays a human action, every month.

What it prepares

## The entries a controller builds by hand every month.

Each entry is balanced, checked against existing entries so nothing is entered twice, and reversible until a person posts it.

### Payroll entries

Payroll journal entries, with the month-end accrual and its reversal when a pay period crosses the month.

- Accrual and reversal
- [Payroll](https://get-lumic.com/services/payroll/)

### Bank reconciliation

Bank-reconciliation adjustments, prepared from the reconciliation the agent runs.

- Bank to book
- Unposted until approved

### Inventory clearing

The inventory clearing entries, prepared for the controller to review.

- Prepared every month
- Unposted until approved

### Monthly adjustments

The monthly adjusting entries, built the same way every month.

- Balanced before staging
- Never entered twice

### Tie-outs first

Bank reconciliation, an intercompany tie-out where a business runs more than one company, and an income-statement tie-out, all before anything is approved.

- Run before approval
- Intercompany where it applies

### A live result

A live income statement, consolidated where there is more than one company, shows the result as soon as the entries post. A monthly management report reads the books live, and the controller finalizes it after month-end.

- Live income statement
