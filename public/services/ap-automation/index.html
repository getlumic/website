Approval and access

Nothing posts without you.

  • It makes the entry itself.

    The agent enters the work in your systems, the same records a clerk would key. A person approves it, and nobody retypes it.

  • Nothing posts without you.

    The agent prepares each entry. A person on your team checks it and approves it, and the final post stays in your hands. Dashboards only read.

  • It touches only its job.

    lumic gives each agent access to the screens and records its job needs, and nothing more. Every action it takes is logged.

  • It runs where it fits.

    Each agent works on its own dedicated computer, wherever fits your business, under its own login, inside the systems you already run. To read a document, it uses a cloud AI model.
