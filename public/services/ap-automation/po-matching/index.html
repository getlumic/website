An AI agent reads each vendor bill, finds its purchase order and compares the prices and quantities. It flags a price or quantity off the PO, or a duplicate invoice number, before anyone pays. A person approves each bill before anything posts. lumic builds it on site and keeps it working.

How the agent matches a bill to the PO.

  2. 01

    It reads the bill.

    The agent reads each vendor bill as it arrives by email, the same bill a clerk would open.

  3. 02

    It finds the PO.

    It finds the purchase order the bill belongs to and compares the prices and the quantities.

  4. 03

    A person decides the rest.

    The bills it can’t match go to a person with the reason. A matched bill still waits for a person’s approval.

Flags before anyone pays.

When a bill does not match its purchase order, the agent flags it for a person before it goes any further.

01

Price off the PO

The bill’s price is not the price on the purchase order. The agent flags the difference for a person to approve.

Quantity off the PO

The bill charges for more or less than the purchase order. The agent flags it for a person to check.

The full AP job: AP automation

Gated by the people who know the work.

  • It makes the entry itself.

    The agent enters the work in your systems, the same records a clerk would key. A person approves it, and nobody retypes it.

  • The final post stays with your team.

    An agent prepares the work. The people on your team who know it approve it before it posts. Dashboards only read.

  • It touches only its job.

    lumic gives each agent access to the screens and records its job needs, and nothing more. Every action it takes is logged.

  • It runs where it fits.

    Each agent works on its own dedicated computer, wherever fits your business, under its own login, inside the systems you already run. To read a document, it uses a cloud AI model.

The practices on every agent: Guardrails.

PO matching, in plain answers.

  • Is this PO matching software?

    No. It is a service, not a product to install. lumic’s FDEs build the agent around how the work is really done on your team, and lumic runs it from there.

  • Which systems does it work with?

    The accounting software or ERP, the inbox the bills arrive in, and the system that holds the purchase orders. Where a system has an API, the agent uses it; where it does not, the agent works through its screens. The list: Systems lumic works with.

  • Who approves each bill?

    The person on your team who approves bills today. The agent only prepares each bill, and any agent can be paused, or its access revoked, at any time.
