# An order desk agent, with a person sending every reply.

The agent reads each purchase order and customer email, drafts the sales order in your systems, and drafts the reply. A person on your team checks each one and sends it. Nobody retypes an order.

How it works

## From the inbox to a drafted order, checked by a person.

### It reads the order.

The agent reads each purchase order and customer email where it arrives: the customer, the items, the quantities, the dates and the ship-to.

### It drafts the order and the reply.

It drafts the sales order in your systems and a reply to the customer, and flags anything that does not match, such as a price or an item it cannot find.

### A person checks and sends.

A person on your team checks each order and each reply, fixes what needs fixing, and sends it. Nothing goes to a customer without that person.

What it handles

## The work that fills the order desk inbox.

lumic’s FDEs start beside the person who does it today, then build the agent around how the work is really done.

### Purchase orders by email.

Attached or in the body, read and drafted as a sales order for a person to check.

### Order status questions.

The agent looks up the order and drafts the answer. A person sends it.

### Changes and exceptions.

A changed quantity, a new ship-to, a price that does not match: flagged for a person, never changed on its own.

### It touches only its job.

lumic gives each agent access to the screens and records its job needs, and nothing more. Every action it takes is logged.
