Privacy, in plain words.

Last updated 12 October 2026

This page explains what information lumic LLC ("lumic") collects through get-lumic.com and when you contact lumic, and what lumic does with it.

This website

  • The site sets no cookies and has no ads or tracking pixels.
  • The site has one form: the checklist form. It stores the email address you enter, the page it was submitted from, your browser type, and the time it was sent. These are kept with Cloudflare, the site’s host.
  • The site’s host, Cloudflare, processes standard request data (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
  • The site loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends a request to Google when a page opens.
  • lumic counts visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies, does not follow you to other sites, and shows lumic only totals, such as page views and which pages people read.

When you email lumic

If you write to an address at get-lumic.com, lumic keeps your message and contact details to reply to you and, if you and lumic work together, to do that work. lumic’s email runs on Microsoft 365.

LinkedIn

lumic uses a LinkedIn developer app only to publish posts to its partners' own LinkedIn profiles and its company page. It does not collect information about other LinkedIn members. When you interact with lumic on LinkedIn, LinkedIn's own privacy policy applies.

What lumic does not do

lumic does not sell your information, and does not share it with anyone except the service providers named above, or when the law requires it.

Client work

Data lumic handles for clients is covered by each client's written agreement with lumic, not by this page.

Your choices

To see, correct, or delete the information lumic holds about you, email hello@get-lumic.com. lumic will reply within 30 days.

Changes

If lumic’s practices change, lumic will update this page and the date above.
