Outlook (Mac or web)

  1. Outlook, Settings, Signatures (on the web: Settings, Account, Signatures).
  2. New signature, name it "lumic", paste.
  3. Set it as the default for new messages and for replies. Save.

iPhone Mail

  1. Open this page in Safari on the phone and tap Copy signature.
  2. Settings, Apps, Mail, Signature. Clear the old text and paste.
  3. If the logo looks wrong, shake the phone and tap Undo once: that keeps the formatting.

Outlook on the phone

  1. Outlook app, your profile picture, Settings, Signature.
  2. Paste. The app may drop the logo; the text stays.
