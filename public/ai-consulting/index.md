## The questions an owner actually has.

### Which job is worth automating?

The job that takes the most hours by hand, and where a mistake costs the most.

### Does it work with the systems the team already runs?

Whether that’s an ERP, accounting software, email, spreadsheets or cloud apps: what the agent would read, and what it would enter.

### Who approves what the agent does?

Where the approval step goes, and who on your team checks each entry before anything posts.

### What does it cost, and how long does it take?

The build, the timeline, and the monthly fee to keep it working, in writing.
