AI agents for accounting: what they do, and how to start.

An AI agent for accounting does one job a clerk does today, such as entering vendor bills or preparing month-end entries, inside the accounting software or ERP you already run. It prepares the entry; a person approves it before anything posts.

The jobs that fit

Repetitive, rule-bound, and checked by a person.

The best accounting jobs for an agent repeat every day or every month, follow rules a controller can write down, and already end in a review.

Controls

Gated by the people who know the work.

  • A person approves every entry.

    The agent prepares each entry. A person on your team checks it and approves it before anything posts, and the final post stays in your hands.

  • Access is limited to the job.

    Each agent works under its own login, with access to the screens and records its job needs, and nothing more.

  • Everything is logged.

    Every action an agent takes is logged, so a reviewer can see what it did and who approved it.

  • Paused or revoked any time.

    Any agent can be paused, or its access revoked, at any time.

Start with the job that takes the most hours.

List the accounting jobs your team does by hand, with the hours each one takes. Pick one. Watch it done, write down the checks and the exceptions, then build the agent around how the work is really done.
