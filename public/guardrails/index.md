The practices

## Approval, logging, access and upkeep.

### A person approves every entry.

The agent prepares each entry. A person on your team checks it and approves it before anything posts, and the final post stays in your hands. A person sends every collections email.

### Everything is logged.

Every action an agent takes is logged. A posted payroll entry is logged with its transaction number, and it can’t be posted twice.

### Access is limited to the job.

lumic gives each agent access to the screens and records its job needs, and nothing more. Each agent works under its own login, on its own dedicated computer.

### Paused or revoked any time.

Any agent can be paused, or its access revoked, at any time.

### Dashboards only read.

Dashboards read live from your ERP. Nothing posts from them.

### A cloud AI model reads documents.

To read a document, such as a vendor bill, the agent uses a cloud AI model.

### Watched and tested every month.

Once an agent is live, lumic watches it, tests it every month, fixes what breaks, and upgrades it as AI improves.

### Client data stays under contract.

Data lumic handles for a client is covered by that client’s written agreement with lumic.
