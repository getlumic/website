The jobs

## What an agent does with Sage Intacct.

### Vendor bills.

The agent reads each vendor bill from email, matches it to the purchase order, and prepares the bill in Sage Intacct for a person to approve. [AP automation](https://get-lumic.com/services/ap-automation/)

### Cash receipts and collections.

Remittances read and keyed as receipts for approval, and collections emails drafted from live aging for a person to send. [Cash receipts and collections](https://get-lumic.com/services/cash-receipts-and-collections/)

### Live reports.

Sales, margin and receivables read live from Sage Intacct, so nobody exports the same report every morning. [Dashboards and reporting](https://get-lumic.com/services/dashboards-and-reporting/)

Nothing posts without you.

### It touches only its job.

lumic gives each agent access to the screens and records its job needs, and nothing more. Every action it takes is logged.

### The final post stays with you.

The agent prepares each entry. A person on your team checks it and approves it, and the final post stays in your hands.
