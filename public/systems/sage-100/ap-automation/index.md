# Sage 100 AP automation, with a person posting every batch.

An agent reads each vendor bill, matches it to the purchase order, and prepares the AP invoice and the PO receipt in Sage 100, in an unposted batch. A person on your team reviews the batch and posts it. lumic builds it on site and keeps it working.

The job, step by step

## From the inbox to an unposted batch. A person posts it.

### A bill arrives by email.

The agent reads each vendor bill as it arrives by email, the same bill a clerk would open.

### It matches the PO.

It matches the bill to the purchase order in Sage 100.

### It prepares the batch.

It prepares the AP invoice and the PO receipt in an unposted Sage 100 batch, ready for a person to review.

Flags

## Flags before anyone pays.

When a bill does not match, the agent flags it for a person before it goes into the batch.

### Price off the PO

The bill’s price is not the price on the purchase order. The agent flags the difference for a person to approve.

- Flagged before anyone pays

### Quantity off the PO

The bill charges for more or less than the purchase order. The agent flags it for a person to check.

- Flagged before anyone pays

### A duplicate invoice number

The vendor’s invoice number is already in Sage 100. The agent flags it, so the same bill is not paid twice.

- Flagged before anyone pays

Access

## Its own computer, its own login.

### Its own computer and login.

The agent works on its own dedicated computer, under its own login, inside the Sage 100 your team already uses.

### Access scoped to AP.

lumic gives the agent access to the AP screens and records its job needs, and nothing more.

### Every action logged.

Every action the agent takes is logged.

The post stays with you.

### A person posts every batch.

The agent prepares the batch and leaves it unposted. A person on your team reviews it and posts it. The final post stays in your hands.

### Nothing posts without you.

The agent never posts the batch itself. Flagged bills wait for a person’s approval.
