# Sage 100 AR automation, with a person approving every receipt.

An agent reads remittances and ACH detail and prepares the cash receipt in Sage 100 for a person to approve. From live Sage 100 aging, it drafts the collections email and the statement for each past-due customer, and a person sends every one. lumic builds it on site and keeps it working.

## From remittance to cash receipt. A person approves it.

### A remittance arrives.

The agent reads the remittance and the ACH detail, the same documents a clerk would key from.

### It keys the receipt.

It prepares the cash receipt in Sage 100, the cash application a clerk would key by hand.

### A person approves it before it posts.

A person on your team checks the receipt and approves it. Nothing posts without that approval.

## Collections drafted from live aging.

The agent reads the aging live from Sage 100 and drafts the collections email and the statement for each past-due customer. A person on your team reviews each one and sends it.

## Its own computer, its own login.

### Its own computer and login.

The agent works on its own dedicated computer, under its own login, inside the Sage 100 your team already uses.

### Access scoped to receivables.

lumic gives the agent access to the receivables screens and records its job needs, and nothing more.

### Every action logged.

Every action the agent takes is logged.

The approval stays with you.

### A person approves every receipt.

The agent prepares each receipt, and a person on your team approves it before it posts. The final post stays in your hands.

### Gated by the people who know the work.

Collections drafts wait for a person to review and send them.

## Sage 100 AR automation, in plain answers.

### Can AI do cash application in Sage 100?

Yes. The agent reads each remittance with a cloud AI model, keys the cash receipt from it, and leaves the approval to a person on your team.

### Does the agent send collections emails on its own?

No. A person on your team sends every one. Any agent can be paused, or its access revoked, at any time.
