How it works

## Built around your NetSuite, not a template.

Every NetSuite account is set up differently: its own subsidiaries, approval routing, custom fields and saved searches. lumic’s FDEs start beside the person who does the job today and build the agent around how your account really works.

### Its own role and login

The agent works in NetSuite under its own login, with access to the records its job needs and nothing more.

- Access scoped to the job
- Every action logged

### Your approval routing

The agent prepares each entry, and a person on your team approves it before anything posts. The final post stays in your hands.

- A person approves each entry
- The final post stays with you

### Across the tools around it

Bills arrive by email, remittances come from the bank, and someone keeps a spreadsheet on the side. The agent works across all of it, not just inside NetSuite.

- Email, bank detail, spreadsheets
- One job, end to end

The jobs

## What an agent does in NetSuite.

### Vendor bills.

The agent reads each vendor bill from email, matches it to the purchase order, and prepares the bill in NetSuite for a person to approve. [AP automation](https://get-lumic.com/services/ap-automation/)

### Cash receipts and collections.

Remittances and ACH detail read and keyed as receipts for approval, and collections emails drafted from live aging for a person to send. [Cash receipts and collections](https://get-lumic.com/services/cash-receipts-and-collections/)

### Live reports.

Sales, margin and receivables read live, so nobody rebuilds the same report every week. [Dashboards and reporting](https://get-lumic.com/services/dashboards-and-reporting/)
