## With an API, or without one.

Most business software has an API, and lumic’s agents use it. Older desktop software, portals and in-house databases often don’t. There, the agent works through the same screens your team uses, on its own computer and login, and checks every step.

## Inside your systems, under its own login.

### It runs where it fits.

Each agent works on its own dedicated computer, wherever fits your business, under its own login, inside the systems you already run. To read a document, it uses a cloud AI model.

### It touches only its job.

lumic gives each agent access to the screens and records its job needs, and nothing more. Every action it takes is logged.

### Gated by the people who know the work.

An agent prepares the work. The people on your team who know it approve it before it posts. The final post stays in your hands. Dashboards only read.

No migration. No new system.

- Instead of → With lumic
- Replacing the system your team knows → An agent that works inside it
- Re-keying between two systems → An agent enters it; you approve
- Build-and-ghost consultants → A team that stays on
