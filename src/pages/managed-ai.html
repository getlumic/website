Why AI needs upkeep

AI looks simple from the outside. It takes upkeep underneath.

An agent depends on connections, rules and checks. The work around it changes: a vendor sends a new bill layout, a system updates its screens, a new kind of exception comes up. AI models improve too. Left alone, an agent drifts; kept up, it gets better.
