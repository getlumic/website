Forward Deployed Engineers for small and mid-size businesses.

A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) works on site with a business, builds inside its own systems, and stays to run what was built. lumic’s FDEs have finance backgrounds. They build one back-office AI agent at a time, inside the systems your team already runs, and keep it working. A person on your team approves every entry before it posts.

{{book}} What an FDE does

An FDE works on site, builds, and stays.

  2. 01

    Works on site.

    lumic’s FDEs work beside your team where the work happens. They map your systems, your data, and the manual steps that take the most time.

  3. 02

    Builds.

    The team builds an agent for one job, connects it to your systems, and sets up the approval step. Your team tests it on real work before it goes live.

  4. 03

    Stays.

    Once an agent is live, lumic runs it for a monthly fee. The team watches it, tests it every month, fixes what breaks, and upgrades it as AI improves.
