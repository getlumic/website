How it works

From the inbox to a drafted order, checked by a person.

  2. 01

    It reads the order.

    The agent reads each purchase order and customer email where it arrives: the customer, the items, the quantities, the dates and the ship-to.

  3. 02

    It drafts the order and the reply.

    It drafts the sales order in your systems and a reply to the customer, and flags anything that does not match, such as a price or an item it cannot find.

  4. 03

    A person checks and sends.

    A person on your team checks each order and each reply, fixes what needs fixing, and sends it. Nothing goes to a customer without that person.
